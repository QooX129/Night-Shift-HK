夜更的士 · NIGHT SHIFT HK

Your streets. Your shift.

A little taxi story on the real streets of Kowloon. Pick up a passenger, find your way, and make the city your own.

Steer with WASD / arrow keys or touch pedals. Hold brake after stopping for low-speed reverse. The taxi stays on the road.

Ordinary junctions highlight exits. Only connected stacked-road forks pause for a choice. You always steer manually.

Stop at the coloured roadside marker to pick up or drop off. Fuel, traffic, and closures are simulated. Buildings have no collisions.

Progress lasts for this page session only. Reloading starts a new shift.

A little care goes a long way.
