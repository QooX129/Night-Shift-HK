The game's district road database and building/park backdrop are adapted from OpenStreetMap. The adapted district database is also offered under ODbL 1.0. Download the adapted database or the bounded original OSM extract. The preparation script is included with the project as tools\prepare_map.py.

本遊戲的道路、建築物及公園資料來自 OpenStreetMap，並經過處理。 經處理的區域資料庫亦以 ODbL 1.0 提供。你可以使用以上連結下載原始資料及經處理的資料庫。

Not a real navigation service / 並非實際導航服務

The experimental 3D renderer uses locally packaged Three.js under the MIT licence. Building footprints come from OpenStreetMap; heights, windows, and the taxi model are artistic approximations. The 3D view does not add real-world elevation or building collision data.

Road outline geometry uses locally packaged polygon-clipping under the MIT licence.

Road data may be incomplete or outdated. Pickup points are fictional game stops near named areas, not verified taxi stands or building entrances. Fares, congestion, closures, fuel, upgrades, and passenger stories are fictional. Do not use this game for real-world driving.

道路資料可能不完整或過時。上落客點屬遊戲設定，並非已核實的的士站或建築物入口。 車資、交通、封路、燃油、升級及乘客故事均為虛構，請勿用作實際駕駛導航。

The data licence does not grant rights to OpenStreetMap trademarks or imply OSM endorsement. Original game code, visual design, and authored dialogue are separate from the OSM-derived database.
