Not a real navigation service / 並非實際導航服務

The experimental 3D renderer uses locally packaged Three.js under the MIT licence. Building footprints come from OpenStreetMap. Tagged heights are used where available; floor-count × 3.2 m and an 18 m default are explicitly labelled estimates, not measurements. Windows and the taxi are artistic.

Elevation sources / 高度資料來源

© Hong Kong SAR Government, Lands Department and DATA.GOV.HK. The district contains a clipped, resampled 5 m DTM and a clipped highest-detail official 3D infrastructure model of the Yau Ma Tei Interchange / Hoi Wang Road pilot crossing. Government data may be downloaded, reproduced and redistributed for commercial and non-commercial purposes, subject to source/IP attribution, indemnity and the DATA.GOV.HK terms and 3D API terms and fair-usage policy. Government data remain under these terms, not the ODbL or the game-code licence.

The DTM accuracy is ±5 m. It includes elevated roads, bridges and vegetation: it is not bare-earth ground beneath flyovers. Ground reconstruction, road profiles outside the pilot, tunnel clearance (5 m), bridge thickness (0.65 m), and vertical alignment of the pilot model are estimates. The pilot is source-model/topology confirmed, not survey verified. Per-feature quality, hashes and source URLs are included in the offline elevation package.

官方地形網格精度為 ±5 米，包含天橋及植被，不能直接推算橋底地面。 試點天橋採用官方三維模型；地面重建、其他道路及隧道高度、樓層換算和垂直基準對齊均為估算，並非實地測量。

Road outline geometry uses locally packaged polygon-clipping under the MIT licence.

Road data may be incomplete or outdated. Pickup points are fictional game stops near named areas, not verified taxi stands or building entrances. Fares, congestion, closures, fuel, upgrades, and passenger stories are fictional. Do not use this game for real-world driving.

道路資料可能不完整或過時。上落客點屬遊戲設定，並非已核實的的士站或建築物入口。 車資、交通、封路、燃油、升級及乘客故事均為虛構，請勿用作實際駕駛導航。
